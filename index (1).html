Beyond ownership01 / collection exposure

A collection can be
more than a collectible.

NFTs connect art, identity and community. STRAPTRADE adds another dimension: a market for the conviction behind a collection.

Instead of requiring a trader to buy or sell an individual NFT, synthetic collection exposure is designed to express a view on the direction of the broader market.

One market, more possibilities03 / participation
01 / Express a view

Trade the direction, not an individual item.

Collection-level synthetic exposure is designed for traders who have a view on an NFT market without needing to select and own one specific collectible.

02 / Hedge exposure

Bring downside tools to a market built around ownership.

Long and short exposure can introduce ways to express risk views that traditional NFT spot marketplaces do not provide.

03 / Use collateral

Put eligible assets to work.

The protocol design describes fungible collateral and an NFT Collateral Vault for NFT-backed trading credit, subject to protocol eligibility and risk controls.
