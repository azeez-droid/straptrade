Collections have communities, conviction and value. What they need next is financial infrastructure.

STRAPTRADE is building a decentralized exchange for leveraged exposure to NFT collections, without buying or selling the underlying NFTs.

THE OPPORTUNITYSTRAPTRADE

The culture evolved.
The tools need to follow.

01 /

Liquidity

Trading an individual NFT depends on finding a buyer for that asset. Collection-level financial exposure offers another way to participate.

02 /

Market access

A trader may have a view on a collection without wanting to own a particular item. A synthetic index separates that view from the choice of collectible.

03 /

Holder utility

The deck proposes collateral and credit functions that let NFTs play a role beyond being bought, held or sold.

WHO IT IS FORSTRAPTRADE

Different perspectives.
A shared market.

01 / COLLECTORS

Keep the connection to your collection.

NFT-backed credit is proposed as a way to access liquidity using an NFT as collateral. Borrowing terms and eligible assets are still to be defined.

02 / TRADERS

Trade the direction, not an individual item.

Synthetic collection indices are designed to support long and short exposure. Positions remain distinct from ownership of the underlying artwork.

03 / COMMUNITIES

A financial layer around shared culture.

The vision is to bring more market tools to the collections people already follow, while keeping collection identity at the center.
