SHORT EXPOSURE

A view that the index will fall.

A short position gains from a downward move in the collection index and loses from an upward move, before fees and funding.

Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. A position can be liquidated if its collateral no longer meets margin requirements.

What sits behind

01 /

Collateral

The whitepaper lists USDC, ETH, SOL and BTC as fungible collateral examples, alongside eligible NFTs deposited into the Collateral Vault and assigned an index-derived collateral value.

02 /

Funding

A periodic funding mechanism redistributes value between long and short positions when directional exposure becomes imbalanced, while execution remains referenced to the NFT Index Price.

03 /

Liquidation

Initial Margin and Maintenance Margin define position health. The design adds TWAP smoothing, oracle deviation checks and volatility-aware controls before its staged liquidation pipeline.

A closer look

Do I own the underlying NFT when I open a position?

No. The proposed perpetual market gives exposure to a synthetic collection index. It does not transfer ownership of an underlying NFT.

How is the collection price determined?

The whitepaper describes a multi-layer oracle architecture using marketplace data, Reservoir, Chainlink and Pyth inputs, followed by filtering and normalization before the NFT index is used by the trading system.

Is this already a live trading system?

No. The whitepaper documents the proposed protocol design and the Product page shows a product concept. The website does not present these mechanisms as deployed markets.
