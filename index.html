A guide to the document

01 /Sections 1–3

Protocol overview

A decentralized perpetual-trading design built for NFT collections. Synthetic exposure separates a trading position from ownership of an individual collectible.

02 /Sections 4 & 7

Collection pricing

The oracle architecture combines marketplace inputs with collection-level valuation. The document covers index construction, input validation and chain-agnostic normalization.

03 /Sections 5 & 10

Perpetual trading

Long and short positions are designed to execute against an index reference. The whitepaper describes trading workflows, funding calculations and settlement.

04 /Section 6

Collateral & risk

Initial and maintenance margin establish the collateral requirements for positions. The proposed risk framework includes leverage controls, collateral valuation and liquidation stages.

05 /Sections 8, 9 & 11

System architecture

Vaults, synthetic exposure, the trading engine and governance form the system modules. The document also covers participant roles, access control and the proposed security framework.

06 /Section 12

Development

The whitepaper includes development and ecosystem plans. For the latest website schedule, see the roadmap beginning with Foundation in Q4 2026.
