The vision

NFTs moved culture.
Now, let’s move the market.

The idea is simple: build financial infrastructure around collection-level exposure without requiring the trader to transfer the underlying NFT.

The opportunity01 / structural gaps

The culture evolved.
The tools need to follow.

Liquidity

Separate market exposure from finding a buyer for one NFT.

Spot NFT markets depend on individual listings and fragmented venue liquidity. A synthetic collection market is designed to create a distinct financial layer around the collection’s value.

Market access

Let a trader express a collection-level view.

A trader may be bullish or bearish on a collection without wanting to own a particular item. Synthetic exposure separates that directional view from the choice of collectible.

Holder utility

Extend what eligible ownership can do.

The whitepaper describes an NFT Collateral Vault intended to support NFT-backed trading credit under protocol-defined valuation and risk rules.

Who it is for02

Different perspectives.
A shared market.

The same collection can matter differently to a holder, a trader and a community. STRAPTRADE is designed around that shared market context.

01 / COLLECTORS

Keep the connection to the collection.

Eligible NFT collateral is described as a way to unlock trading credit without selling the asset, while withdrawals remain subject to solvency requirements.

02 / TRADERS

Trade direction instead of selecting one NFT.

Synthetic collection indices are designed to support long and short exposure, leverage, funding and margin logic without transferring the underlying collectible during trading.

03 / COMMUNITIES

Build a financial layer around culture.

The long-term vision is to add market infrastructure to NFT ecosystems while keeping the identity of each collection visible rather than turning every market into an anonymous ticker.
