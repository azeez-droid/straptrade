01 /The vision

NFTs moved
culture.
Now, let’s move
the market.

Collections have communities, conviction and value. What they need next is financial infrastructure.

Why this matters

The culture evolved.
The tools should follow.

01 / Liquidity

More ways to participate.

Trading an individual NFT depends on finding a buyer for that asset. Collection-level exposure creates another way to express a market view.

02 / Market access

Conviction beyond ownership.

A trader may follow a collection without wanting to own a particular item. A synthetic index separates a directional view from the choice of collectible.

03 / Holder utility

A broader role for a collectible.

The protocol design includes NFT collateral and NFT-backed credit, extending the potential use of an NFT beyond buying, holding or selling.

Who it is for

Different views.
Shared conviction.

Collectors

Keep your connection.

NFT-backed credit is designed to let holders access liquidity through eligible collateral. Collateral remains subject to valuation and liquidation rules.

Traders

Express your perspective.

Take long or short exposure to a collection index. A trading position is separate from ownership of the artwork.

Communities

Build around culture.

Financial infrastructure can give communities more ways to engage with the collections they already follow.
