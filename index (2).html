Collateral
Approved fungible assets or NFT-backed credit support a position. The design applies valuation and eligibility rules to the collateral committed.
Funding
Periodic funding is designed to align perpetual exposure with the collection index. The whitepaper describes the calculation and how market imbalance affects payments.
Liquidation
If account equity falls below maintenance margin, a position becomes eligible for liquidation. The design includes partial and full position closure.

A closer look.

Does opening a position mean I own the NFT?

No. A position provides synthetic exposure to a collection index. It does not transfer ownership of an underlying NFT.

What does support across all chains mean?

The valuation model is designed to work across chains using reliable oracle feeds and native-asset normalization. Launch networks and eligible collections remain subject to implementation and market selection.

Can I trade on STRAPTRADE today?

Not yet. The site presents the protocol design and product concept. The Trade page will remain marked Coming soon until the product is ready.
