Protocol questions

Where does collection pricing come from?

The whitepaper names NFT marketplace and aggregator inputs including OpenSea, Magic Eden and Reservoir, together with decentralized base-asset networks such as Chainlink and Pyth. It also describes anomaly filtering, stale-feed rejection, TWAP smoothing and liquidity weighting.

Do positions transfer the underlying NFT?

No. The perpetual exposure described in the whitepaper is synthetic. Trading operations are designed around the NFT index rather than transferring the underlying NFT for every position.

How is leverage managed?

The document describes dynamic leverage scaling based on collection volatility and liquidity profiles, with margin and liquidation controls used to manage account solvency.
